Full-service Middle Eastern carriers generally enforce generous baggage limits on standard economy tickets, often allowing between twenty-five and thirty-five kilograms of checked luggage or a two-piece allocation.

However, promotional internet fares occasionally restrict baggage allowances or apply strict weight caps to carry-on items. When itineraries involve connecting regional carriers on separate tickets, baggage allowances rarely carry over automatically. Booking unified tickets under recognised carrier partnerships guarantees that baggage checked in Auckland transfers seamlessly through to the final destination, protecting travellers from exorbitant excess baggage fees at intermediate check-in counters.

Immigration guidelines must also be confirmed well before departure. However, visa rules vary significantly for foreign residents and onward transit destinations.
