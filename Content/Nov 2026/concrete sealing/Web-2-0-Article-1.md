## The Critical Importance of Substrate Repair and Crack Remediation

A concrete slab must provide a sound, level, and structurally unified base before receiving an overlay or coating. Hairline cracks and settlement fissures are not merely aesthetic blemishes. These openings compromise the structural integrity of the floor and allow moisture to penetrate into the concrete matrix.

Standard cosmetic patching using water-based mortar rarely withstands dynamic movement or vehicle loads. Professional remediation requires mechanical diamond V-routing, in which specialised blades square off fractures and clear away fractured concrete edges. Once the fissures are thoroughly cleaned with cyclonic vacuum extractors, technicians inject low-viscosity structural epoxy or polyurea compounds to weld the concrete walls together.

## Mitigating Moisture Vapour Transmission and Hot-Tyre Pickup

A frequent cause of coating delamination in Canterbury garages is moisture vapour transmission. Ground moisture continually migrates upward through porous concrete slabs, creating hydraulic pressure beneath impermeable surface coatings. If an epoxy system is applied over moist concrete without a moisture-mitigating primer, hydrostatic pressure will cause blisters and premature peeling.

Another prevalent failure mode associated with low-grade DIY paint kits is hot-tyre pickup. When vehicle tyres travel along regional motorways, tyre friction generates considerable heat. When the hot tyres park on a weak paint coating, the heat softens the synthetic polymers, causing the coating to stick to the rubber and tear away upon departure.

Commercial installation methods resolve both vulnerabilities. Certified technicians conduct electronic moisture testing across the slab before installation. When elevated moisture is detected, specialised epoxy moisture barriers are applied to block vapour migration. Coupled with deep mechanical grinding, commercial 100% solids epoxy resins permanently withstand thermal stress and tyre plasticisers.
