## Protecting Rural Waste Systems and Septic Tank Microbiology

For rural properties and lifestyle blocks across New Zealand that rely on private septic tanks or aerated on-site wastewater systems, chemical selection is a matter of critical infrastructure protection. Conventional household detergents frequently contain harsh synthetic chemicals, including chlorine bleach, ammonia, phosphates, and synthetic antibacterial compounds.

When flushed into a septic tank, these aggressive chemicals kill off the beneficial anaerobic bacteria responsible for digesting solid organic waste. Without active microbial colonies, septic tanks fill prematurely with undigested solids, leading to clogged drainage fields, foul perimeter odours, and expensive emergency pump-outs.
