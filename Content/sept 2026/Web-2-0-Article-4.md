## Essential Travel Tips and Cultural Etiquette in the UAE

Visiting the United Arab Emirates requires travellers to respect local cultural traditions, legal regulations, and climatic conditions:

1. **Visa and Entry Requirements:** New Zealand passport holders generally receive a complimentary visit visa upon arrival at UAE international airports. Confirm that your passport possesses at least six months of remaining validity from your scheduled arrival date.
2. **Medication Regulations:** The UAE enforces strict regulations regarding the importation of certain prescription and over-the-counter medications. Check whether your personal medications require prior online registration with the UAE Ministry of Health before packing them.
3. **Appropriate Dress and Public Etiquette:** Respect local customs by dressing modestly in public areas, shopping malls, and government offices. Avoid public displays of affection and obtain permission before photographing local residents or government infrastructure.
4. **Hydration and Climate Management:** Desert temperatures can be extreme during the summer months from June to September. Plan outdoor sightseeing activities for early mornings or evenings, and maintain continuous hydration throughout your stay.

Observing these practical guidelines ensures that your visit to the United Arab Emirates remains enjoyable, respectful, and trouble-free.
