## The Mechanical Process of Concrete Densification and Sealing

Transforming an ordinary concrete slab into a high-performance commercial floor requires a precise multi-stage process. Technicians begin by inspecting the slab for moisture vapour emissions, structural cracking, and surface irregularities. Any existing coatings, adhesives, or contaminants must be mechanically stripped away using coarse diamond grinding tooling.

Once the bare aggregate and concrete paste are exposed, liquid silicates or siliconate densifiers are evenly applied across the slab. These reactive chemical compounds absorb into the capillary pores of the concrete. A chemical reaction transforms soft calcium hydroxide into hard calcium silicate hydrate crystals, effectively locking the surface against abrasion and liquid ingress.

Following densification, progressive grinding stages refine the floor to the desired sheen level. Finally, penetrating sealers or topical micro-sealers are buffed into the surface. This comprehensive process ensures the finished floor resists oil spills, acidic liquids, and tyre marking while maintaining slip-resistant traction under dry and wet conditions.
