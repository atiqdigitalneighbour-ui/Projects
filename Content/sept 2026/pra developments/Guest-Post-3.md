## Geographic Positioning and Multi-Modal Transit Connectivity

The investment appeal of Belmont is underpinned by its geographic position. Bounded by the Swan River to the north and west, the suburb offers scenic waterfront parklands alongside direct arterial connections. Major transit thoroughfares, including Great Eastern Highway, Tonkin Highway, and Leach Highway, provide seamless connectivity to industrial hubs, retail centres, and metropolitan employment zones.

Public transportation infrastructure adds another layer of tenant appeal. Regular high-frequency bus services connect Belmont directly to the Perth central business district, whilst the Redcliffe passenger rail station provides rapid transit links to the airport and eastern corridors. This comprehensive transit network appeals to corporate professionals, shift workers, and university students seeking convenient, car-independent commuting options.
