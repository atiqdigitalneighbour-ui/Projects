## The Rise of Multi-Generational Living and Flexible Zoning

Accommodating several generations under a single roof requires floor plans that go beyond traditional suburban templates. Ground-floor guest suites with private en-suite bathrooms have become essential for elderly family members who prefer to avoid navigating staircases. Concurrently, designated home offices or study sanctuaries prevent work activities from encroaching upon relaxation spaces.

Acoustic management represents another vital consideration for large family residences. Positioning bedrooms away from central living hubs, open-plan kitchens, and home entertainment rooms preserves domestic harmony. Modern multi-level homes resolve these challenges by separating active communal zones on the lower level from quiet sleeping retreats on the upper storey.
