Natural citrus cleaning concentrates derive their exceptional cleaning potency from d-limonene, an organic compound cold-extracted from orange and lemon rinds. Unlike synthetic detergents that simply float on top of soil, citrus terpenes act as potent natural solvents that dissolve oily bonds on molecular contact.

When applied to stubborn kitchen grease, baked-on stovetop splatters, or oily fingerprints, citrus solvents penetrate the soil structure immediately. The organic grease dissolves into the liquid emulsion, allowing it to be wiped away cleanly with a microfibre cloth without intense manual scrubbing.

Furthermore, citrus terpenes are naturally biodegradable and non-toxic. They break down rapidly into harmless organic substances through natural microbial action, making them safe for homes equipped with septic tanks or greywater irrigation systems. Unlike harsh synthetic bleaches or caustic oven cleaners, natural citrus solutions release no toxic chemical fumes, leaving indoor living areas filled with a refreshing, authentic orange scent.

## Custom Dilution Guidelines for the Home

The versatility of citrus concentrates allows homeowners to tailor dilution strengths to match specific domestic cleaning challenges:

- **Heavy-Duty Kitchen Degreasing:** Mix one part concentrate with five parts warm water to dissolve thick grease on range hoods, barbecue hotplates, stovetops, and garage tool surfaces.
- **General Multi-Surface Maintenance:** Blend one part concentrate with twenty parts water for daily wiping of dining tables, kitchen benchtops, sealed timber cabinets, and painted doors.
- **Glass and Mirror Polishing:** Use a light dilution of one part concentrate to fifty parts water to achieve a streak-free, crystal-clear shine on windows and bathroom mirrors.
- **Hard Floor Mopping:** Add a single capful of concentrate to a warm mop bucket to clean sealed hardwood, ceramic tiles, and vinyl flooring without leaving sticky residues behind.
