## The Process of Achieving a Polished Concrete Finish

Professional concrete polishing is not a single-step application. It involves a sequence of mechanical grinding passes using progressively finer diamond tooling. Each pass removes the previous surface scratches and refines the finish until the concrete reaches the desired level of sheen.

The process typically begins with coarse diamond segments to open the concrete surface and remove any existing coatings, contamination, or high spots. Subsequent passes use medium and then fine grit tooling to bring the surface through a series of refined stages. A chemical hardener is applied partway through the process to densify the concrete and improve its resistance to abrasion and staining.

The final result can range from a matte honed finish to a high-gloss mirror-like surface, depending on the number of polishing stages and the grit used in the final pass. The choice of finish is typically driven by the intended use of the floor and the aesthetic preferences of the client.
