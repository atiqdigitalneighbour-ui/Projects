## The Polishing Process Explained

Professional concrete floor polishing follows a logical sequence of steps. The process begins with coarse diamond tooling that removes any surface contamination, existing coatings, or high spots and opens the concrete to the desired depth. This initial grinding phase also determines how much of the aggregate is exposed in the finished floor.

Subsequent passes use progressively finer grit tooling to refine the surface and reduce the scratch marks left by each previous pass. A chemical densifier is applied during this stage to harden the concrete and reduce its porosity. This densification step is critical to achieving a floor that resists staining and holds its polish under sustained use.

The final passes bring the surface to the specified sheen level, whether that is a low-sheen honed finish or a high-gloss mirror surface. The choice of finish should reflect the intended use of the space and the personal preferences of the property owner.
