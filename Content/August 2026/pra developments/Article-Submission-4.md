## Essential Inspection Tips for Three-Bedroom Buyers

When evaluating potential three-bedroom properties, buyers should consider the following practical elements:

1. **Storage Capacity:** Confirm that all bedrooms include built-in robes and check for additional linen cupboards and garage storage areas.
2. **Natural Lighting and Ventilation:** Ensure main living spaces and bedrooms feature generous windows to capture natural sunlight and coastal breezes.
3. **Garage and Parking Security:** Look for a secure double garage with direct internal shoppers' access for safety and convenience.
4. **Energy Efficiency Features:** Check for quality thermal insulation, double glazing, and efficient LED lighting to ensure low ongoing utility bills.

By prioritising these features, buyers in Belmont can secure a beautiful, versatile three-bedroom home that delivers exceptional everyday comfort and long-term financial security.
