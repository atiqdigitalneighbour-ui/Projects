## Comparing AGM and Heavy-Duty Flooded Chemistries

Understanding the physical construction of lead-acid power units clarifies why specific vehicle classes require tailored internal chemistry. Standard flooded commercial units utilise liquid sulphuric acid electrolyte surrounding thick antimony or calcium-alloy plates, offering economical cyclic endurance and simple top-up capabilities for managed transport fleets.

In contrast, modern performance motorcycles and severe-service fleet vehicles increasingly depend on sealed AGM technology. By immobilising liquid acid within porous glass mats, AGM batteries eliminate the danger of acid leakage during aggressive cornering or steep off-road work. Furthermore, sealed constructions accept replenishment current much faster than traditional flooded batteries, allowing short journeys to restore depleted reserves effectively.
