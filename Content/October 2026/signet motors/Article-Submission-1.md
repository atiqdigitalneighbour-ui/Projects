## The Japanese Auction Grading Architecture

Before an automobile is exported from Japan, it undergoes evaluation at major Japanese wholesale auto auctions. Highly trained independent assessors examine the vehicle thoroughly, recording structural condition, panel damage, paint deterioration, and interior wear on a standardised auction sheet.

Auction grades are assigned using a clear numerical and alphabetical scale:

- **Grade 5.0 and Grade 4.5:** Denotes near-new condition with minimal mileage, pristine factory bodywork, and flawless interior trim.
- **Grade 4.0:** Represents exceptional pre-owned quality with minor superficial blemishes consistent with gentle use, and zero structural chassis repairs.
- **Grade 3.5 and Grade 3.0:** Indicates moderate to significant wear, noticeable panel damage, previous paint repairs, or superficial rust blemishes.
- **Grade R or RA:** Indicates vehicles that have undergone structural accident repairs to chassis rails, core supports, or pillar sections.

While budget-oriented volume dealers frequently acquire Grade 3.0 or repaired Grade R vehicles to minimize initial costs, quality-focused dealerships source exclusively from Grade 4.0 and above. This rigorous selection criterion eliminates vehicles with compromised collision histories or hidden structural corrosion.

## Statutory Entry Certification and Structural Compliance

Upon disembarkation at New Zealand ports, imported vehicles must clear mandatory biosecurity inspections before entering statutory compliance facilities. Authorised inspection organisations, including VTNZ and the AA, conduct comprehensive technical verifications mandated by the New Zealand Transport Agency.

Certified compliance inspectors dismantle vehicle sill covers and examine the underside chassis on commercial vehicle hoists. They verify structural alignment, evaluate steering component tolerances, measure brake rotor and pad thicknesses, and ensure all factory seatbelt webbing assemblies operate correctly. Only vehicles that pass these rigorous structural and safety criteria receive a New Zealand entry compliance certificate.

This statutory inspection confirms that the vehicle is physically fit for registration on New Zealand roads, providing purchasers with fundamental safety assurance.
