# The Essential Guide to Interior Concrete Polishing: Aggregate Exposure, Sheen Levels, and Daily Care

Polished concrete has transformed modern residential design, moving from commercial warehouses into contemporary homes and architectural villas. Homeowners and interior designers appreciate its seamless visual flow, exceptional durability, and natural thermal efficiency.

Unlike traditional flooring products that require regular replacement, polished concrete is an integral structural component of the building. Understanding the customisation options available, from aggregate exposure to reflectivity, allows homeowners to achieve the ideal aesthetic for their interior living spaces.

---

## Defining Aggregate Exposure Levels

Every concrete slab contains an underlying matrix of sand, cement paste, and coarse aggregates. During the mechanical diamond grinding phase, technicians can control how deeply they cut into the surface paste, dictating the volume of aggregate revealed:

- **Cream / Nil Exposure:** The diamond tooling removes only the thinnest microscopic layer of surface laitance, leaving the underlying stones concealed. The finished surface displays a smooth, industrial stone appearance with subtle colour swirls and character marks.
- **Salt and Pepper Exposure:** Light surface grinding shaves away one to two millimetres of paste, exposing fine sand particles and tiny stone specks. This clean, understated finish suits minimalist and Scandinavian interior palettes.
- **Medium to Full Exposure:** Deep grinding removes three to five millimetres of concrete paste, revealing heavy cross-sections of colourful regional gravels and basalt river stones. This finish resembles traditional terrazzo and provides exceptional camouflage for daily household dust.

---

## Selecting the Desired Sheen Level

Following aggregate exposure and chemical densification, the slab is polished through successive passes of resin-bonded diamond pads. Homeowners can select the level of surface reflectivity that best complements their interior lighting:

- **Matte or Honed Finish:** Polished to approximately 200 to 400-grit, this finish offers a soft, non-reflective surface that diffuses light gently across casual spaces.
- **Satin Finish:** Refined to 800-grit, a satin polish delivers a subtle, lustrous sheen that reflects ambient daylight without creating sharp reflections. This is a popular choice for family living rooms and open-plan kitchens.
- **High-Gloss Finish:** Polished to 1,500 or 3,000-grit, high-gloss concrete acts as an optical mirror, bouncing overhead lighting and window sunlight throughout the home to maximize interior brightness.

## Energy Efficiency and Underfloor Heating Performance

Beyond visual sophistication, polished concrete offers outstanding thermodynamic performance. Concrete possesses high thermal mass, absorbing heat during sunny daylight hours and releasing it gradually as evening temperatures drop.

When paired with in-slab hydronic radiant heating systems, polished concrete acts as an immediate thermal conductor. Unlike carpets, underlays, and timber floating floors that insulate against heat transfer, polished concrete allows warmth to radiate unhindered across living spaces.

This high thermal conductivity allows heat pumps to operate at lower, more efficient water temperatures. As a result, households enjoy uniform radiant warmth throughout chilly southern winters whilst reducing monthly electrical utility costs.

## Everyday Maintenance and Long-Term Preservation

Polished concrete is remarkably straightforward to maintain, yet basic preventative care ensures the crystalline surface retains its brilliance for decades:

1. **Dust Mopping:** Use a dry microfibre mop daily to remove loose sand and grit that could cause micro-abrasions under foot traffic.
2. **Damp Mopping:** Clean weekly using warm water and a neutral-pH floor cleaner. Avoid acidic cleaning agents such as citrus or vinegar solutions, which can chemically etch the surface paste over time.
3. **Prompt Spill Cleanup:** While penetrating stain guards provide transient protection, clean spills of wine, coffee, and acidic oils promptly to prevent surface marking.
4. **Protective Furniture Pads:** Attach felt protectors beneath heavy chairs and tables to prevent surface scratching during movement.
