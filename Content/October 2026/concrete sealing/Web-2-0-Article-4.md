## Energy Efficiency, Ambient Light Reflectivity, and Workplace Safety

A compelling financial advantage of polished commercial concrete is its ability to enhance interior illumination. High-gloss polished surfaces act as a natural optical reflector, bouncing natural daylight from skylights and perimeter windows across the shop floor.

By maximizing light diffusion, retail operators can lower artificial overhead lighting power levels without sacrificing visual brightness. This reduction in electrical power consumption yields significant operational savings over multi-year commercial leasing periods.

Additionally, polished concrete delivers outstanding slip resistance despite its glassy, reflective appearance. When polished according to industry standards, dry densified concrete maintains a high coefficient of friction that meets New Zealand building compliance codes.
