# Compact Living in Perth: Why 1-Bedroom Properties in Belmont Are Worth Considering

The appeal of compact residential properties has grown significantly across Perth's inner suburbs over the past several years. For a diverse range of buyers — from FIFO workers seeking a low-maintenance base near the airport, to retirees downsizing from large family homes, to investors pursuing strong yield from a manageable asset — 1-bedroom and compact-format properties represent a category that deserves serious consideration.

Belmont, situated just seven kilometres east of the Perth CBD, has emerged as a suburb where compact living works particularly well. The combination of infrastructure, location, and housing mix creates conditions that favour purposeful, well-designed smaller properties.

## Who Actually Buys or Rents Compact Properties in Belmont?

Before examining what to look for in a Belmont 1-bedroom property, it is worth understanding who occupies these dwellings and why. The demand side of the market is more diverse than many buyers initially expect.

### FIFO and Shift Workers

Perth Airport is one of Western Australia's largest employment nodes. Workers on fly-in fly-out rosters from the resources sector, along with aviation maintenance, cargo, and airline operations staff, collectively represent a substantial workforce that prioritises proximity to the airport terminals above almost everything else.

For these buyers and tenants, a compact, secure, low-maintenance property that is seven minutes from the airport — with a double lock-up garage, automated reticulation, and modern air conditioning — is far more valuable than a larger family home in a distant suburb. The lock-and-leave quality of a well-designed townhouse means that during extended rosters away from Perth, the property requires minimal attention. On return, it offers immediate comfort without any maintenance backlog to address.

### Retirees and Downsizers

Perth has a substantial population of older residents who continue to occupy large family homes on quarter-acre blocks long after their children have left. The practicalities of managing a large property — lawns, gardens, ageing plumbing, roof maintenance, and the physical demands of upkeep — eventually prompt many to consider downsizing.

For these buyers, a well-designed 1-bedroom or compact 2-bedroom townhouse offers a genuine lifestyle upgrade. Modern construction means better thermal performance, lower energy bills, and appliances under warranty. A courtyard with automated reticulation requires a fraction of the maintenance that a large suburban garden demands. And the capital released from selling a large established home can fund a more comfortable retirement without ongoing maintenance anxiety.

### Young Professionals and Students

Perth's inner suburbs attract young professionals working in the CBD, health sector, and hospitality industry who want to minimise commute times without paying western-suburbs prices. A 1-bedroom property in Belmont provides a sensible compromise: near-city access at an accessible price point with a modern specification.

### Property Investors

For investors, compact properties in Belmont offer the combination of strong yield potential and manageable acquisition cost. Perth's rental vacancy rate has remained well below 1%, which means tenant placement in well-located properties tends to be straightforward. Gross rental yields recorded in Perth's inner-east suburbs have been above 6%, a figure that significantly outpaces most comparable properties in Sydney or Melbourne.

A 1-bedroom property generates lower absolute rental income than a larger dwelling but often delivers a comparable or superior yield percentage relative to its acquisition cost. For investors building a portfolio progressively, smaller entry points can provide earlier market access with lower individual risk exposure.

## What Makes a Well-Designed 1-Bedroom Property Functional?

Not all compact properties are created equal. The difference between a well-designed and a poorly designed 1-bedroom home is felt every day by the occupant, and it directly affects how quickly the property tenants and how long tenants stay.

### Spatial Efficiency

A well-designed compact home makes every square metre count. Generous ceiling heights create a sense of volume that overcomes the constraints of a smaller footprint. Open-plan kitchen and living arrangements prevent the cramped feel that closed-off room arrangements can produce. Storage that is integrated into the structure — built-in wardrobes, under-stair storage, kitchen pantry space — reduces the reliance on external furniture.

### Natural Light

A compact property with generous natural light feels larger than its footprint suggests. Glazed external doors opening to a private courtyard or balcony, along with well-positioned windows that draw morning or afternoon light through the living areas, are design features that distinguishes quality compact developments from minimum-standard products.

### Private Outdoor Space

Even in a 1-bedroom configuration, private outdoor space significantly increases liveability. A courtyard, terrace, or balcony that is usable — not just a token strip of paving — allows occupants to benefit from Perth's exceptional outdoor climate without requiring a large garden to maintain.

### Secure and Low-Maintenance

A single lock-up garage and secure building access are features that tenants in this category consistently prioritise. For FIFO workers spending extended periods away, security provides peace of mind. For investors, secure properties tend to attract more cautious and reliable tenants.

## Why Belmont's Location Suits Compact Living

Location is the one element of a property that cannot be changed after purchase. For compact living to succeed over the long term, the suburb must compensate for the smaller dwelling size through excellent access to services and employment.

Belmont achieves this for several reasons.

Perth Airport is minutes away by car, which matters enormously to the FIFO worker and aviation workforce demographic that is a natural fit for compact Belmont properties. The Swan River foreshore, immediately to the south, provides walking and cycling infrastructure that residents can access without a car. Belmont Forum Shopping Centre provides comprehensive retail and healthcare services within the suburb itself.

The CBD is reachable in under twenty minutes by car in most traffic conditions, and road connections via the Great Eastern Highway and the Roe Highway extension give residents multiple routing options. For tenants who work across a range of locations rather than a single fixed office, this flexibility in commute routing is genuinely valued.

## Evaluating a 1-Bedroom Belmont Development Before Buying

Compact properties require particularly careful evaluation because the margin for poor design or low-quality construction is smaller than in a larger dwelling. Every decision in a 1-bedroom home is visible and consequential.

Buyers and investors should assess:

**Construction quality and materials:** New properties built to current National Construction Code standards offer better insulation, more energy-efficient glazing, and structural standards that older Belmont homes do not meet. For investors, higher-quality construction means lower ongoing maintenance costs.

**Developer track record:** A developer who has completed comparable compact developments in Perth can demonstrate their ability to execute the product correctly. Request details of completed projects and, where possible, arrange to inspect them.

**Inclusions and turnkey delivery:** A compact property that requires post-handover expenditure on landscaping, air conditioning, or window treatments is not genuinely ready for occupancy or tenancy. Confirm the full inclusions schedule in writing before signing.

**Strata structure and levies:** Many compact developments operate as Survey-Strata titles with minimal shared infrastructure. Confirm the levy structure and what it covers to ensure ongoing costs are predictable.

## Lock-and-Leave Living: A Genuine Lifestyle Choice

The phrase "lock-and-leave" is used often but its true meaning is worth unpacking. A genuine lock-and-leave property is one where the owner can depart with no outstanding tasks, no maintenance to arrange, and confidence that the property is secure and self-sustaining in their absence.

This requires automated reticulation so gardens do not require manual watering. It requires a secure, enclosed garage rather than an open carport. It requires modern security features on doors and windows. And it requires building materials that do not demand regular painting, sealing, or structural attention in the short to medium term.

For the FIFO worker heading back to the Pilbara, or the retiree spending winter months interstate, this level of self-sufficiency is not a luxury. It is a functional requirement that affects the property's suitability for their lifestyle. When evaluating compact properties in Belmont, the lock-and-leave standard is a useful filter that quickly distinguishes well-considered developments from those built to minimum requirements.
