# Why 4-Bedroom Homes Remain in High Demand Across Perth's Inner Suburbs

Perth's inner suburban residential market has experienced sustained demand pressure across most dwelling types. Within that broader trend, 4-bedroom properties occupy a particularly strong position. They attract a wide range of tenants, deliver favourable investment metrics relative to smaller dwellings, and offer genuine functional value for owner-occupiers who need space without the cost of a large outer-suburban block.

Understanding why 4-bedroom homes perform consistently well in Perth's inner suburbs helps buyers and investors assess whether this configuration suits their goals.

---

## The Trend Toward Larger Townhouses for Families and Investors

Perth's urban planning environment has changed significantly over the past decade. State government policy encouraging infill development within established suburbs has made it more viable to construct larger, multi-level townhouses in well-connected inner and middle-ring locations.

This planning shift has benefited buyers who previously faced a binary choice: a small apartment in an inner suburb, or a large house on a big block in an outer suburb. Today, a family seeking a 4-bedroom home can access that configuration in Rivervale, Belmont, or Kewdale without commuting from a distant outer suburb.

For investors, the same dynamic has opened access to a dwelling size with strong tenant demand characteristics in locations that previously offered mainly smaller apartment stock.

## Why 4-Bedroom Configurations Attract Premium Tenants

The type of tenant attracted to a 4-bedroom property in Perth's inner east is typically a stable, longer-term occupant. This matters for investors because tenant stability directly affects the consistency of rental income and the frequency of re-letting costs.

Families with school-age children tend to value school proximity, yard space, and stability. They are reluctant to move frequently and, when they find a property that meets their needs, tend to stay for extended periods. A well-designed 4-bedroom townhouse with a private courtyard and access to good schools and public transport is exactly the product this demographic seeks.

Professional households — dual-income couples, housemates, or family units with a home-office requirement — also seek 4-bedroom configurations that allow for dedicated work and guest spaces. In a post-pandemic rental market where working from home remains common, the demand for an extra bedroom to serve as a study has expanded the tenant pool for 4-bedroom properties.

## Urban Infill and Space-Efficient Larger Homes

Urban infill development in Perth's established inner suburbs has enabled a generation of well-designed 4-bedroom townhouses that use space efficiently. Multi-level layouts separate living and sleeping zones, which appeals to both families and professional households. Open-plan ground-floor arrangements connect kitchen, dining, and living spaces to private outdoor areas.

These designs achieve meaningful floor area — sufficient for genuine 4-bedroom living — without requiring the large land footprint of a traditional suburban house. This space efficiency makes 4-bedroom townhouses financially viable on infill sites in established suburbs, which is why this product type has grown in availability in Rivervale, Belmont, and Kewdale over recent years.

For buyers considering this property type, the spatial quality of the floor plan is as important as the raw bedroom count. A 4-bedroom home where the fourth bedroom is barely large enough to hold a single bed is functionally different from one where all four bedrooms are appropriately sized. Reviewing the floor plan in detail, and where possible inspecting comparable completed developments, is an important step in the assessment process.

## The Role of Tax Depreciation for Investors

New 4-bedroom properties in Perth's inner suburbs offer significant tax depreciation benefits that older established homes cannot match. Australian tax legislation allows investors in new residential properties to claim deductions against taxable income through two depreciation mechanisms.

Division 40 depreciation applies to plant and equipment within the property: kitchen appliances, air conditioning systems, carpet, and similar items. On a new 4-bedroom property, the full cost of all new plant and equipment qualifies for depreciation from the date of settlement.

Division 43 depreciation applies to the structural capital works of the building itself. The ATO allows a deduction of 2.5% of the original construction cost per year for forty years on eligible residential properties. For a brand-new 4-bedroom townhouse, the full construction cost qualifies for this deduction from the date of first use.

The combined impact of these deductions can be significant. Investors who commission a quantity surveyor's depreciation schedule after settlement will have a clear picture of the annual deductions available. For investors in higher income tax brackets, these deductions can meaningfully improve the after-tax cash flow of the investment in its early years.

## What to Look for in a 4-Bedroom Townhouse Layout

Not all 4-bedroom floor plans are equally liveable. Buyers assessing competing developments should evaluate the following layout elements.

**Bedroom sizing:** All four bedrooms should be proportioned to accommodate standard bedroom furniture comfortably. Ask for the room dimensions in the floor plan document rather than relying on visual impressions from a render.

**Bathroom ratio:** A 4-bedroom home with a single bathroom creates congestion in a multi-occupant household. Two bathrooms — one typically associated with the master suite — is the practical minimum for a family or professional group household.

**Living and outdoor connection:** An open-plan ground-floor living space that connects directly to a private courtyard or alfresco area expands the usable living area and improves cross-ventilation, which is particularly important in Perth's climate.

**Garage:** A double garage or two allocated car spaces is the appropriate minimum for a 4-bedroom dwelling. Single garage configurations are a meaningful limitation for households with two vehicles or for tenants who value secure parking.

**Storage:** Integrated storage throughout the property — walk-in wardrobes, linen cupboards, under-stair storage — reduces the need for bulky freestanding furniture and improves the liveability of the space.
