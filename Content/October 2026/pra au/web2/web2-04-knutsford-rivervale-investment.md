## A Suburb Profile Worth Understanding

Rivervale sits approximately five to eight kilometres east of the Perth CBD. It borders the Swan River to the south and connects directly to the Belmont, Burswood, and Ascot precincts. Residents have straightforward access to the Graham Farmer Freeway and Great Eastern Highway, which keeps travel times into the city predictable.

Perth Airport is also within close range, a detail that matters considerably for FIFO workers and frequent travellers. The airport's proximity adds a layer of practical convenience that many inner-east suburbs share, and it is one reason why rental demand in this corridor tends to hold up across different economic conditions.

The suburb is served by local retail strips, parklands along the river, and access to the broader amenities of the Belmont and Burswood areas. For buyers who want inner-ring convenience without the premium prices of western suburbs, Rivervale represents a sensible middle ground.

## How Buyers Should Approach Due Diligence on Off-the-Plan and New Builds

Buying a new or recently completed property requires a different approach to research than purchasing an established home. Buyers should focus on several specific areas.

### Construction Quality and Warranty Cover

One of the most important questions for any new build purchase is what happens after settlement. In Western Australia, residential building work must be covered under the Home Indemnity Insurance framework, but buyers should also look for developers who offer additional warranty assurances. A six-year Australian-Standard structural warranty, for example, provides a meaningful layer of protection well beyond the handover date.

### Fixed Pricing and Cost Transparency

Cost blowouts during construction are a well-documented risk for buyers who engage builders under variable or provisional pricing arrangements. A fixed-price contract locks in the total cost of the build, which removes the exposure to unexpected variations. For first-home buyers managing a tight budget, this structure can make the difference between a manageable purchase and a stressful one.

### Developer Track Record and Accessibility

Researching a developer's completed projects gives buyers a clearer picture of delivery standards and construction quality. It is also worth assessing how accessible the developer is during the process. Buyers who can speak directly with decision-makers, rather than working only through sales representatives, often find that issues or questions are resolved more efficiently.
