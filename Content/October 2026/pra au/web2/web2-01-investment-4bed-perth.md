# What Perth Property Investors Should Know Before Buying a 4-Bedroom Investment Property

Buying an investment property in Perth is a significant financial decision, and the type of dwelling you choose matters more than many investors realise. Four-bedroom properties occupy a distinct position in the rental market — one that rewards investors who understand tenant demand, location dynamics, and the true cost of ownership. This article outlines the key considerations before committing to a 4-bedroom investment in Perth's current market.

## Why 4-Bedroom Properties Perform Well as Rentals

Perth's rental market has been under sustained pressure for several years. With a vacancy rate sitting below 1% across much of the metropolitan area, tenants are competing for quality homes. In this environment, 4-bedroom properties consistently attract a wider pool of reliable, longer-term tenants.

Families with school-age children, dual-income professional households, and FIFO workers sharing a property between swing rosters all gravitate towards 4-bedroom homes. These tenant profiles tend to stay longer, pay consistently, and treat the property as a genuine home rather than a temporary arrangement. Lower tenant turnover means fewer vacancy gaps and reduced re-letting costs over time.

A 4-bedroom home also gives tenants the flexibility they cannot find in smaller dwellings — a dedicated home office, a guest room, or space for growing families. That added functionality supports stronger rental demand even when broader market conditions soften.

## Location: Why Perth's Inner-East Suburbs Deserve Attention

Not all Perth suburbs offer the same investment fundamentals. Proximity to the CBD, transport infrastructure, employment hubs, and lifestyle amenities all influence both rental demand and long-term capital growth potential.

Suburbs in Perth's inner-east corridor — including Rivervale, Belmont, and Kewdale — sit 5 to 8 kilometres from the Perth CBD. This positions tenants within easy reach of the city without the premium land costs associated with western suburbs. For investors, this means a more accessible entry point without sacrificing the location attributes that attract quality tenants.

### Airport Proximity and FIFO Demand

Perth Airport is one of the city's largest employment precincts, and its proximity to the inner-east suburbs makes this corridor particularly appealing to FIFO workers and aviation industry employees. Workers on rotating rosters often prefer to rent rather than own, and a well-appointed 4-bedroom home near the airport is exactly the type of property they seek.

### METRONET and Public Transport Access

The ongoing expansion of Perth's METRONET rail network continues to improve connectivity across the metropolitan area. Improved rail access strengthens the liveability of inner-east suburbs and supports rental demand from tenants who rely on public transport to commute. Investors who purchase in well-connected locations are better placed as infrastructure improvements filter through to the surrounding property market.

### Lifestyle and Amenities

Rivervale, Belmont, and Kewdale all offer established retail, dining, and recreational infrastructure. Proximity to the Swan River foreshore, major shopping centres, and sporting facilities adds genuine lifestyle value — a factor that tenants weigh heavily when choosing where to live.

## The Case for Turnkey Delivery in Investment Property

One of the most overlooked costs in investment property is the time and money spent getting a newly built or renovated home to a rentable standard. Investors frequently underestimate finishing costs, landscaping, window treatments, appliances, and the weeks of rental income lost while a property sits incomplete between construction and tenancy.

A fully turnkey property eliminates this problem entirely. When a home is delivered move-in ready — with landscaping, flooring, fixtures, appliances, and all finishes completed — it can go to market the day keys are handed over. For investors, this directly protects rental income from day one.

Fixed-price turnkey delivery also removes the financial uncertainty that plagues cost-plus building contracts. When the price is locked in from the outset, investors can model their returns accurately without allowing for budget overruns. For those purchasing from interstate or managing an investment portfolio remotely, this certainty is not a luxury — it is a necessity.

## Understanding Rental Yields in Perth's Inner East

Rental yield is one of the primary metrics investors use to assess a property's income-generating potential. In Perth's inner-east suburbs, gross rental yields have been tracking above 6%, a figure that compares favourably against many east-coast markets where yields have compressed significantly as prices rose faster than rents.

Yield calculations on a 4-bedroom investment property need to account for the full purchase and delivery cost, not just the land and construction price. Stamp duty, legal fees, landscaping, and any post-handover works all affect the effective yield. This is precisely why a transparent, all-inclusive pricing structure is important when comparing properties.

## Questions to Ask Before Committing

Before signing a contract on a 4-bedroom investment property in Perth, investors should work through a structured set of questions:

- **Is the price truly fixed?** Confirm in writing that there are no provisional sums, prime cost items, or post-contract variations that could inflate the final cost.
- **What is included at handover?** A fully turnkey property should include landscaping, driveway, window treatments, floor coverings, and all kitchen and laundry appliances.
- **What warranty applies?** A 6-year structural warranty aligned with Australian Standards provides meaningful protection on a newly completed property.
- **Who is managing the project?** Direct access to the developer or builder's directors — rather than being passed through a sales intermediary — allows investors to resolve queries efficiently and maintain visibility over the build.
- **What is the timeline to rental income?** Understand the expected settlement date and how quickly the property can be listed with a property manager after handover.
