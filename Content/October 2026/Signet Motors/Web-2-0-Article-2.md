## Decoding Japanese Auction Grading and Structural Verifications

In Japan, commercial vehicle auctions utilise an internationally recognised grading matrix to evaluate vehicle condition. These independent auction inspections evaluate structural integrity, paint condition, interior cleanliness, and mechanical operation. Vehicles are scored on a numerical scale, where Grade 4.0, Grade 4.5, and Grade 5.0 denote superior condition with negligible wear and zero structural damage history.

Lower-grade vehicles, classified under Grade 3.0 or Grade 3.5, often feature repaired chassis sections, extensive surface imperfections, or high interior deterioration. While discount retailers frequently purchase these lower-tier vehicles to lower their acquisition costs, reputable traders adhere strictly to Grade 4.0 and higher.
