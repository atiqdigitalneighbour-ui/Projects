# Essential Travel Preparation for Flights from Auckland to Delhi

Delhi serves as the primary international gateway to northern India, connecting global visitors with historic landmarks, cultural centres, and extensive domestic transit networks. ## Navigating Immigration and Visa Protocols

Entering India requires international visitors to hold valid travel authorisations. Most foreign nationals, including New Zealand citizens, must apply for an Indian electronic visa prior to travelling. Processing times vary, so applications should be lodged well in advance of departure dates.

In addition to destination entry rules, passengers must ensure that intermediate transit stops do not require transit visas. Certain airport hubs enforce transit visa criteria based on passport nationality or connection duration. ## Luggage Strategies for Long-Distance Travel

Packing for extended visits to India requires careful attention to airline luggage weight thresholds. International airlines generally offer checked baggage allowances of twenty to thirty kilograms, or two checked bags of twenty-three kilograms each, depending on fare class.

Ensuring that all flight sectors are booked under a single ticket guarantees that baggage allowances carry through from Auckland to Indira Gandhi International Airport. Furthermore, luggage transfers automatically across connecting flights, relieving travellers of collecting and re-checking heavy suitcases during layovers.

## Arrival and Ground Transit in Delhi

Indira Gandhi International Airport operates Terminal 3 as its primary international facility. Terminal 3 features modern amenities, foreign currency exchange counters, and reliable transport connectivity, including the direct Delhi Airport Metro Express into central New Delhi.

Travellers arriving during late evening hours should arrange pre-paid airport taxi services or pre-booked hotel transfers to ensure safe and predictable transit to their accommodation. Keeping digital and printed copies of flight itineraries, visa authorisations, and hotel reservations simplifies airport exit procedures.
