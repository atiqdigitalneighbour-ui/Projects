## The Compelling Economics of Adding Your Own Water

The core difference between pre-diluted cleaning sprays and professional concentrates is active ingredient density. Supermarket sprays are heavily diluted to hit low initial retail price points, but their high turnover rate makes them expensive over time. In contrast, concentrated cleaning solutions contain concentrated active cleaning compounds that users dilute to match specific tasks.

A single bottle of concentrated cleaner can yield dozens of refill spray bottles for daily maintenance. This refill model dramatically lowers the per-litre cost of ready-to-use cleaning solution, delivering substantial financial savings over months and years of use.

Beyond financial savings, concentrates deliver significant environmental benefits throughout the supply chain. Shipping concentrated liquids requires far less fuel, significantly reducing transport-related carbon emissions compared to hauling heavy water-filled bottles. Reusing a durable spray bottle multiple times also prevents dozens of single-use plastic containers from entering municipal landfills.

## Natural Solvency: How Citrus Terpenes Dissolve Tough Grime

The exceptional grease-cutting power of citrus concentrates comes from d-limonene, an organic terpene cold-extracted from the rinds of oranges. Unlike synthetic detergents that merely float on top of soil or require harsh caustics to break down grease, citrus terpenes act as natural solvents that dissolve oily bonds on molecular contact.

When sprayed onto stubborn kitchen grease, baked-on stovetop splatters, or sticky soap scum, citrus terpenes penetrate the soil matrix immediately. The organic fats dissolve into the liquid emulsion, allowing dirt to be wiped away cleanly with a microfibre cloth without intense manual scrubbing.

Furthermore, citrus terpenes are completely biodegradable and non-toxic. They break down naturally into harmless organic substances through microbial action, making them safe for rural homes equipped with septic tanks, greywater irrigation, or sensitive drainage setups. Unlike harsh synthetic bleaches or caustic cleaners, natural citrus cleaners release no toxic chemical fumes, leaving indoor living areas filled with a refreshing, authentic orange scent.
